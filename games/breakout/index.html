Game Center

How to play

Goal: bounce the ball off your paddle to wipe out every brick on the wall. Miss the paddle and you lose a life — you have three.

The rules

  • Bricks are worth more the lower the row — the bottom row pays 70, the top row just 10.
  • Clear the whole wall to advance a level: the ball speeds up and the paddle gets a little narrower.
  • The ball starts parked on the paddle — launch it when you’re ready.
  • Out of lives and the run is over; your best score is kept.

Controls

  • Mouse / touch slides the paddle; ←→ or AD work too.
  • Space or click launches the ball.
  • P pauses · N new game · R rules · M mute.
Your run — score, lives, level and the surviving bricks — is saved in this browser, so a refresh picks up right where you left off.
