Stack the falling blocks, clear the lines.

How to play

Goal: steer the falling tetrominoes into the well and complete full horizontal rows to clear them — the higher your stack climbs, the closer you are to game over.

Controls

  • ← / → or AD move the piece left or right.
  • ↑ or W rotates the piece clockwise.
  • Hold ↓ or S to drop the piece faster — applies to the current piece only.
  • Space pauses — it also starts a game after a run ends; R starts a new one — or use the on-screen pad on touch devices.

Rules

  • Pieces fall in a randomised “7-bag” order — all seven shapes appear before any repeats.
  • Clearing 1–4 rows scores 100 / 300 / 500 / 800 × level.
  • Every 10 lines raises the level, and the pieces fall faster.
  • A piece locks when it can’t fall further; if a new piece can’t spawn, the game is over.
  • Your best score and the current run are saved in this browser.
