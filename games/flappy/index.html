How to play

Goal: steer the bird through the gaps between the pipes for as long as you can. One touch = one point. Gravity is forever.

The rules

  • Each tap gives the bird an upward flap; it falls right after.
  • Pass a pipe pair to score 1 point — the gaps get a little tighter and the world speeds up the further you go.
  • Clip a pipe, the ground or get crushed from above: run over.

Controls

  • Click / Tap / Space flap.
  • N restart · R rules · M mute.
Your best score is saved in this browser. No accounts, no leaderboards — just you versus the pipes.
