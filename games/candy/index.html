Sweet Crush

Score 0 Moves 0 Best 0

How to play

Goal: trade places with two neighbouring candies to line up 3 or more of the same colour. Clears cascade — falling candies make new matches, and new matches pay again.

Making candies

  • Match 4 in a line → a striped candy is born. Clearing it later wipes its whole row or column.
  • Match 5 in a line → a colour bomb. Swap it with any candy to vaporise every candy of that colour.
  • Swap a bomb with another bomb → the whole board goes. Have a nice day.
  • Specials set off each other in chain reactions.

Controls

  • Drag a candy towards a neighbour, or click two neighbours to swap them.
  • Swaps that make no match snap back. No moves left? The board reshuffles itself.
