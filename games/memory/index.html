Game Center

How to play

Goal: flip the cards two at a time and find every matching pair in as few moves — and as little time — as you can.

The rules

  • Click any face-down card to flip it, then flip a second one.
  • Identical cards? The pair stays face-up and locks in place.
  • Different? Both flip back — remember where they were.
  • Each two-card attempt counts as one move; the timer starts on your first flip.

Difficulty

  • 6 pairs — a quick warm-up · 10 pairs — the classic · 15 pairs — a full 5×6 wall of cards.
  • Pairs are real playing-card ranks (e.g. two Queen of Spades tiles), shuffled fresh every game.

Extras

  • Best shows your fastest clear for the current difficulty, saved per board size.
  • Shortcuts: N new · R rules.

Records are saved in this browser — no account, no ads.
