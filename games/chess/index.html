How to play

Goal: checkmate the enemy king — attack it so it has no legal escape.

The pieces

  • Pawn ♙ one step forward (two from home); captures diagonally.
  • Knight ♞ in an “L”; the only piece that jumps.
  • Bishop ♟ any distance diagonally.
  • Rook ♜ any distance in a straight line.
  • Queen ♛ any distance in any direction.
  • King ♚ one step; may castle once with a corner rook.

Special moves

  • Castling: king shuffles two squares toward a rook (allowed when neither has moved and the path is safe).
  • En passant: a pawn may capture a just-double-stepped pawn as if it moved one square.
  • Promotion: a pawn reaching the far rank becomes a queen / rook / bishop / knight.

Playing

  • Click a piece to see its legal moves, then click a highlighted square to go there.
  • Use Undo, Flip, or play vs Computer (Easy / Medium / Hard).
