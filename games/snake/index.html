How to play

Goal: steer the snake to eat as many dots as you can — it grows one segment each time, so don't bite yourself.

Controls

  • Arrow keys or WASD to turn.
  • Swipe on the screen, or tap the on-screen pad, on touch devices.
  • Space pauses; once you’ve crashed it starts a new run.
  • Choose a Speed (Slow / Normal / Fast) any time — it’s saved for next visit.
  • R also starts a new game.

Rules

  • Hitting a wall or your own tail ends the run.
  • Each dot is worth 10 points and the game speeds up as you grow.
  • Your best score and current run are saved in this browser.
