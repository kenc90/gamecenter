How to play

Goal: pilot a ship through a neon tunnel that bends, twists and speeds up forever. One touch of the wall and the run is over.

The rules

  • The tunnel curves on its own — the wall slides into you; steer to stay centered.
  • Orange hazard blocks float in the tunnel — they only block part of it, so steer around them. There is always room to pass.
  • Gold rings are worth 50 points apiece; distance earns 10 points per meter as it grows.
  • Speed creeps up the deeper you fly. There is no finish line — only how good your reflexes are.

Controls

  • ←↑↓→ or WASD — thrust.
  • Mouse / touch — the ship chases your pointer.
  • P pauses · N new game · R rules · M mute.
Your run is saved in this browser every few meters — close the tab mid-flight and the tunnel will be right where you left it. The tunnel itself is generated from your distance, so it rebuilds exactly the same.
