How to play

Goal: ride an endless sunset highway through every checkpoint before the countdown hits zero. Each one you smash adds seconds; the road only gets curvier.

The rules

  • Every run starts behind the 3·2·1·GO! lights — the clock only starts when they go out.
  • Checkpoints add +8 seconds and they are far apart — you have to actually drive to reach them. Miss the clock and the run is over.
  • Traffic is slower than you on purpose. Rear-end it and you lose 4 seconds off the clock, most of your speed, and your line. Ramming your way through is a losing strategy.
  • The grass is not road. Off-track you bleed speed and gain dust.
  • Curves throw you outward — brake early, or the scenery brakes for you.

Controls

  • ↑W accelerate · ↓S brake · ←→ or AD steer.
  • On touch: hold the screen to gun it and drag left / right to steer.
  • P pause · N new run · R rules · M mute.
The highway is generated from your run’s seed, so the same curves are waiting in the exact same place when you come back — the traffic, however, has moved on without you.
