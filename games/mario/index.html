Super Plumber

Press Space (or Start) to play

←→ move · ↑/Space jump · Shift run · P pause

How to play

Goal: run and jump through the stage, grab coins, bonk question blocks from below, stomp the grumpy mushrooms, and reach the flagpole to unlock the next stage.

Controls

  • ← / → or AD to run left or right.
  • ↑, W or Space to jump — hold it longer to jump higher.
  • Hold Shift to sprint.
  • P pauses; R restarts the stage; Esc closes menus.
  • On touch devices use the on-screen pad.

Stages

  • Five stages: 1-1 through 1-5, each harder than the last.
  • Reach the flagpole to finish a stage and take your score into the next one.
  • Use the Stage button in the header to jump straight to any stage.

Tips

  • Jump on an enemy to squash it; touch it from the side and you lose a life.
  • Falling into a pit also costs a life — you start with 3.
  • Your progress and best score are saved in this browser.
