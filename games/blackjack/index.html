How to play

Goal: beat the dealer without going over 21. Get closer than the dealer and double your bet.

A round

  • Pick a chip to set your bet, then press Deal.
  • You and the dealer each get two cards — the dealer's second stays face-down.
  • Cards are worth their number, face cards are 10, and an Ace is 11 or 1.

Your moves

  • Hit — take another card. Go past 21 and you bust and lose the bet.
  • Stand — keep your total and let the dealer play out.
  • Double — double the bet, take exactly one card, then stand.

Payouts

  • Dealer must hit to 17 and stand on all 17s.
  • Win pays 1:1; a natural Blackjack (Ace + 10 on the deal) pays 3:2.
  • A tie is a push — your bet comes back.

Extras

  • New Bank resets you to $500 anytime — this is just for fun, no real money.
  • Shortcuts: Space deal · H hit · S stand · D double · R rules.
