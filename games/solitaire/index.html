How to play

Goal: move all 52 cards to the four foundations (top-right), each built up by suit from Ace to King.

The layout

  • Stock (top-left): click to deal cards to the waste.
  • Waste: the drawn cards you can play from.
  • Foundations: four piles, build up in suit (A→2→3…→K).
  • Tableau: seven columns, build down in alternating colours (e.g. a black 8 on a red 9).

Moving cards

  • Drag & drop a card (or a valid run) onto a tableau column or foundation. A yellow outline marks a legal spot.
  • Double-click a card to send it straight to a foundation if it fits.
  • Only a King (or a run starting with a King) may fill an empty tableau column.
  • When a face-down card is uncovered it flips face-up automatically.

Drawing

  • Use Draw 1 / Draw 3 to choose how many cards the stock deals at once.
  • Click the empty stock (↻) to recycle the waste back into the stock.

Extras

  • Undo reverts the last move; Hint highlights a possible move.
  • Once every card is face-up the game auto-finishes for you.
  • Shortcuts: N new · U undo · H hint · Space draw · R rules.

Your game is saved automatically — you can refresh or come back later and pick up where you left off.
