How to play

Goal: descend an endless mine shaft as deep as you can — the deeper you dig, the more floors you bank.

The squeeze

  • A crushing ceiling slides down from above the whole run, bristling with spikes. It speeds up the deeper you go.
  • Touch those spike tips and you are crushed — instant death, on the ground or mid-air, full health or not.
  • Staying alive means keeping below it, which means keeping down.

The shaft

  • You only move left and right — gravity does the rest. Bounce off the walls if you slide too hard.
  • The shaft is lined with short floating shelves, one to three per row — weave through the open gaps between them to keep dropping.
  • Spike shelves (bristling with pillards) drain your HP bar while you stand on them — at zero HP, you're paste. Landing on a normal shelf heals you back a little.
  • Conveyor belts — the steel ones with amber chevrons — drag you sideways toward the walls while you ride them.
  • Bounce pads — teal springs with up-arrows — sling you back up the shaft. Thrilling, right up until the ceiling reminds you which way is down.

Controls

  • ← → or A D — dig left / right (hold both halves of the screen on touch).
  • Space start · P pause · N new dig · R rules · M mute.
Best depth and a mid-dig save are kept in this browser — close the tab and the shaft will (reluctantly) let you continue.
