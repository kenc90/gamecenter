帥

Chinese Chess

Red to move.

How to play

Goal: trap the enemy General so it cannot escape. Checkmate — or even stalemate — wins the game.

The pieces

  • General 帥/將 one step orthogonally inside the palace; the two Generals may never face each other down an open file.
  • Advisor 仕/夁 one step diagonally, always inside the palace.
  • Elephant 相/象 two steps diagonally, never crossing the river; blocked by a piece at its “eye”.
  • Horse 骕 one orthogonal then one diagonal; blocked by a piece at its “leg”.
  • Chariot 车 any distance orthogonally (like a rook).
  • Cannon 烁/爆 moves like a Chariot but captures by jumping exactly one screen piece.
  • Soldier 兹/单 one step forward; once past the river it may also step sideways. Never backward.

The board

  • Pieces sit on the line intersections; the river divides the two halves.
  • The 3×3 palace (marked with diagonals) confines each General and its Advisors.

Playing

  • Tap a piece to see its legal moves, then tap a highlighted point.
  • Use Undo, Flip, or play vs Computer (Easy / Medium / Hard) — you are Red.
  • Shortcuts: N new · U undo · R rules.
  • The game is saved in this browser — close the tab and come back.
