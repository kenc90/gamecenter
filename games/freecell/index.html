How to play

Goal: move all 52 cards to the four foundations (top-right), each built up by suit from Ace to King. Every deal is solvable.

The layout

  • Free cells (top-left, ✦): four parking spots, one card each.
  • Foundations: build up in suit (A→2→3…→K).
  • Tableau: eight columns, build down in alternating colours.
  • All cards start face-up — nothing to uncover here.

Moving cards

  • Drag & drop a card (or a valid alternating run) onto a column, cell or foundation.
  • Double-click a card to send it straight to a foundation if it fits.
  • Any card or sequence may move to an empty column.
  • How long a sequence you can move depends on empty cells + empty columns: each doubles your reach. (1 + cells) × 2columns cards at once.

Extras

  • Auto ships every safe card home once all your cards are face-up and the cells are free.
  • Undo reverts the last move; Hint highlights a possible one.
  • Shortcuts: N new · U undo · H hint · A auto · R rules.

Your game is saved automatically — refresh any time and pick up mid-deal.
